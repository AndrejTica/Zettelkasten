Scrum is a light-weight framework to help organisations deliver value through
adaptive solutions. 
In short, scrum should push an environment where a scrum master helps with the
following:

- A product owner defines work and puts it into the backlog. 
- Helps the team to create an increment from this backlog.
- Helps the team validate the result of the increment and feed the feedback
back into the next increment. 
- repeat. 

Scrum only defines the minimum rule sets so that the goal can be achieved.
It purposely leaves out many details vague, since it does not want to give out
dogmas. It wants to emphasize people and interactions over detailed processes. 

Scrum is based on empiricism and lean thinking. Lean thinking reduces waste and
focuses on what really matters. 

Scrum is based on an iterative approach to optimize predictability and risk.
It puts people of all types of skill required to complete some work. 

Its based on 3 empirical pillars:

1. Transparency
The work needs to be transparent to everyone, as decisions are made based on
this. Artifacts that are not transparent, are wasteful. 

2. Examination
The work needs to be examined after it has become transparent. Are we moving in
the right direction? This allows for adaptation. As soon as we learn something
new, we need to adapt. Everyone from the teams should be empowered to do so. 
Artifacts that are not transparent, are wasteful.

Scrum values:

## Scrum team

- A scrum team consists of a scrum master, product owner and developers.
- They posses all skills together to accomplish a task (interdisciplinary).
- The team organizes itself. Who will do what and when?
- Typically team size is max 10 people. Small enough for good communication and
  big enough to accomplish meaningful tasks. 
- If too big, should be split into multiple scrum teams but they share the PO
- The scrum team is responsible for all activities relating to the product.
- Each spring should have a sustainable speed of work. 
- The whole scrum team is responsible to deliver a working increment each week.

### Developers
Can have many skills but generally should be able to:
- Create a sprint backlog. 
- Keeping quality by fulfilling the definition of done.  

### Product owners
- Developing the product and explicit communication. 
- Create the product backlog
- Define priorities and make the product backlog transparent and understood.

The decision of the PO has to be respected, if there are differing opinions then
changing the backlog will need convincing work.

### Scrum master
Is responsible for keeping up with scrum values and rules as defined. This is
done by education the team of the scrum values. 
Is responsible for the effectiveness of the scrum team. 
- Teaches self organisation and collaboration. 
- Helps keeping the focus on important increments after the definition of done.
- Helps solve impediments. 
- Makes sure all scrum events are being kept and within the timebox. 
- Helps the PO with managing the backlog. 
- Helps defining clear and concise backlog entries. 
- Helps with empiric product planning. 
- Facilitating the collaboration with stakeholders. 
- Teaching about the values of scrum

## Events

The sprint is a container for all other events. Every event in scrum serves 
the purpose of verifying the scrum artifacts. These events facilitate
transparency. If an event does not happen, an opportunity is missed where
adaptations can be made. The goal is also to minimize any other meetings that
are not defined by scrum. 

### Sprint 
Sprints are the main part of scrum. 
- No changes are made that could divert from the sprint goal.
- Meanwhile no quality should be lost. 
- Product backlog can be changes if new things are learned during the sprint. 

Sprints allow for predictability, because they are evaluated at least each
month. 
Smaller sprints around 2 weeks should be used to have a smaller feedback loop
and thus reduce risk. 

There are good tools to predict the progress, like the [Burn-Down-Charts](Burndown%20chart.md), [Burn
Up-Charts](Burndown%20chart.md#burnup-chart) or [Cumulative-Flow-Diagramme](Cumulative-Flow-Diagramme.md), but they don't replace empiricism. 

The product owner is the one who can cancel the sprint of the sprint goal
becomes obsolete.

#### Sprint planning
- Starts the sprint and defines what will be done in the sprint.
- The product owner takes the most important backlog items and explains them. 
There are 3 main topics in a spring: 
1. Why is this sprint valuable?
2. What can be defines as "done" in the sprint?
3. How will the defines work be done?
- Only developers define how they will do their work, no one else. They split up
  the work into packets that can be done in one day approximately.  

### Daily scrum
Asses the progress and make adaptations to the sprint backlog if needed.
A 15 minute event. Daily scrum improves communication, finds blockers, and
eliminate the need for other meetings.
